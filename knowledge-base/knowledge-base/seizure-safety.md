# Seizure Safety

## During a Seizure

- Stay calm and stay with the person.
- Keep the person away from nearby objects that could cause injury.
- Cushion the person's head if possible.
- Do not restrain the person.
- Do not put anything in the person's mouth.
- Time the seizure.

## After a Seizure

- Stay with the person and allow them to recover.
- When appropriate, place the person on their side.
- Continue to observe their breathing and recovery.
- Record useful observations, such as how long the seizure lasted.

## When Emergency Help May Be Required

Emergency medical help may be required when:

- The seizure lasts more than five minutes.
- Another seizure begins before the person recovers.
- The person has difficulty breathing or does not regain consciousness.
- The person is seriously injured.
- The seizure is a first-time seizure.

## Relevance to Neurovia

Neurovia can use this information as educational
safety guidance alongside its alert workflow.

An alert from the prototype must not be treated as
a medical diagnosis or as a replacement for emergency
medical services.

## Important Limitation

Neurovia is a prototype and is not clinically validated
to diagnose or predict seizures.
