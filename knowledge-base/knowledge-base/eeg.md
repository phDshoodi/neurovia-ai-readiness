# EEG Monitoring

## Overview

EEG (electroencephalography) is a method used to record
electrical activity in the brain.

## Relevance to Neurovia

Neurovia uses EEG sensing as part of its proposed
wearable monitoring workflow.

## Neurovia Prototype

The Neurovia concept uses EEG signals as an input
for signal processing and AI-based risk assessment.

## Important Limitation

The Neurovia prototype should not be interpreted as
a clinically validated EEG-based seizure prediction
system.
