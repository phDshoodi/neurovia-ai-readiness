# EEG Monitoring

## What is EEG?

Electroencephalography (EEG) is a method used to
record the electrical activity of the brain.

EEG is an important test used in the evaluation of
people with seizures and epilepsy.

## EEG and Epilepsy

EEG can support the diagnosis and classification
of epilepsy and can provide information about
abnormal electrical activity in the brain.

However, EEG results must be interpreted by
appropriately trained healthcare professionals.

## Relevance to Neurovia

Neurovia proposes using EEG signals as an input
within its prototype monitoring and AI workflow.

The prototype uses simulated data for demonstration
and does not claim clinical validation.

## Important Limitation

Neurovia's prototype should not be interpreted as
a clinically validated EEG-based seizure prediction
system.
